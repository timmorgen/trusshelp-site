Advances in Truss Repair Guidance Following Severe Wind Events

February 2026

Recent industry guidance has expanded on best practices for evaluating and repairing wood trusses following severe wind events. Emphasis has been placed on proper damage assessment, engineered repair design, and coordination with truss manufacturers and qualified design professionals.

These updates aim to reduce improper field repairs, improve structural performance, and ensure long-term safety of residential and light commercial structures.
